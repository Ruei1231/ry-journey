Georgia in November

Spring, before the crowds

This trip was simple: arrive in the middle of night in Kutaisi, before the tour buses, before anyone else had the same idea. For four mornings in a row I set an alarm I didn't want to hear, and for four mornings in a row it was worth it — Kyoto in late autumn belongs, briefly, to whoever is willing to be there first.
