Desert Silence

A night with no signal and no light pollution

There's a particular kind of quiet that only exists a long way from anywhere. Replace this opening with your own first impression of the desert — the heat of the day giving way to the cold of the night, or the moment the stars actually became visible.

Describe the camp, the people, the food, the small rituals of a night spent somewhere without electricity. This kind of trip often has very little "plot" — the writing can lean entirely on atmosphere and sensory detail instead.

End on the silence itself, or whatever the desert left you thinking about once you were back somewhere loud again.
